## Typeface

[Manrope](https://github.com/sharanda/manrope), weights 200 to 800. Headings and the wordmark use ExtraBold (800). Manrope is licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 and is not covered by the licence of this repository.

## How to write the name

- In text: **Diagnelia**, with a capital D.
- In the logo the wordmark is set in lower case, **diagnelia**. Do not retype it, use the files above.
- Slogan: **Your story, finally heard.**

## Please do not

- change the colours, proportions or shape of the logo
- stretch, rotate, outline or add effects to it
- redraw the mark or set the wordmark in another typeface
- place logo.svg on a busy or dark background (use logo-on-dark.svg instead)

For press, partner materials or any other use, email [info@diagnelia.com](mailto:info@diagnelia.com).
